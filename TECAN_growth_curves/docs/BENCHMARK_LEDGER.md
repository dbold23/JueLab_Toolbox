This file accounts for every point of movement between the synthetic benchmark
the repository published before the suite rebuild (**v1**) and the one it
publishes now (**v2**). Each number below names the command that reproduces it.
Run every command from the repository root with `MPLBACKEND=Agg` set.

**No pipeline threshold changed.** The rebuild is confined to the generator,
the ground truth, the validator and the ML training script. Exactly two
pipeline files changed since `827d95e`, both additively (WP9):

* `scripts/01_growth_curve_analysis.py` — a `failed_gates` column appended
  **last** to `processing_results.csv`, and a `run_settings.json` sidecar
  written beside it;
* `tecan_growth/analyze.py` — the same column added to `RESULT_COLUMNS`,
  `result_row` and `legacy_results_frame` (the lab-interface path).

`scripts/config.yaml` changed only in its comments. Section 6 gives three
independent proofs that no verdict and no number moved.

### Nothing is hidden by the scoring policy

179 of the 1139 curves are not in the headline because their `scoring_mode`
says the current gate set cannot decide them fairly. They are scored here
anyway. Two rules are in play, and each number below names the one it comes
from:

* **flat rule** — the headline's own rule, applied unchanged: a curve is
  correct when the pipeline's accept/reject verdict matched the truth's
  GOOD/BAD. Every block is scored this way.
* **block rule** — a second, stricter rule that only `a_not_identifiable`
  carries, recorded in `report.json` at
  `truncation.a_not_identifiable.rule`: correct if the pipeline rejected the
  curve, **or** the fitted A is within 15 % of the observed final OD and no
  more than 1.20 × the last observed point. It asks a question the flat rule
  does not — whether the *reported amplitude* is supportable from a run that
  stopped before the plateau.

| block | n | flat rule correct | flat accuracy | block rule correct | block-rule pass rate | why it is out of the headline |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `a_not_identifiable` | 80 | 68 | 0.850 | 12 | 0.150 | run stops before the plateau, so A is not identifiable. The *verdict* is right on 68 of the 80 — the truth calls these curves GOOD and the pipeline accepted them — while the *reported A* is supportable on only 12. The 0.850 is not a defence of the block and the 0.150 is not the pipeline getting 80 % of them wrong; they answer different questions, which is exactly why the block sits outside the headline. |
| `preprocessing_blind` | 99 | 24 | 0.242 | — (no block rule) | — | `write_legacy_csvs` hands the pipeline the replicate mean only, so a per-well artefact is invisible to it |
| **all 1139, flat rule, nothing excluded** | **1139** | **933** | **0.8191** | — | — | balanced accuracy 0.8128, sensitivity 0.8605, specificity 0.7652 |

The generated `VALIDATION_SUMMARY.md` prints both `a_not_identifiable`
numbers in the same two-row table, so the two documents state the same pair
and neither can be read as the block's single score.

The flat 0.8191 is the pessimistic bound. It is published here so the headline
cannot be read as a selection.

## 2. The signed decomposition

Each row is a defined, measurable intermediate on one path from the v1 headline
to the v2 headline. Rows 1-3 are counterfactuals on the **v1 suite** (480
curves, so accuracy is out of 480); rows 4-5 are **measured** on the v2 suite.
Row 4 is the residual line: whatever rows 2-3 do not explain.

| # | cause | effect on correct verdicts | running accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | v1 as published | 419 / 480 | **0.8729** |
| 1 | label rule -> `oracle` on `borderline_noise`, `borderline_r2_bad`, `high_noise` | **+23 to +27** (audit's naive count: +35) | **0.9208 – 0.9292** (naive: 0.9458) |
| 2 | logistic converter fix (`from_gompertz_params` now realises the shape) | **+13** | **0.9479 – 0.9563** (naive: 0.9729) |
| 3 | *(subtotal: the two causes above, zero pipeline change)* | 455 – 459 / 480 | 0.9479 – 0.9563 |
| 4 | **residual on the core 32** — everything else the rebuild changed there: baranyi/richards/haldane converters, `borderline_delta_od` restored to its configured range, noise recalibration, content-addressed reseeding, per-scenario counts | **+0.42 to +1.26 pp** | **0.9605** (511/532, measured) |
| 5 | 32 new scenario families enter the headline (428 curves, accuracy 0.7710) | **−8.45 pp** | **0.8760** (841/960, measured) |
| 6 | *(memo)* fold the 179 policy-excluded curves back in under the flat rule | −5.69 pp | 0.8191 (933/1139, measured) |

**Row 1, in full.** The audit counted the 35 v1 false positives and stopped
there. Relabelling those scenarios also *breaks* the verdicts the pipeline got
right under the old label, and an honest count subtracts them:

| scenario | n | v1 label | v1 correct | pipeline said GOOD | v2 `oracle` split | relabelled correct | net |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `borderline_noise` | 15 | BAD (all) | 1 | 14 | 15 GOOD / 0 BAD | 14 | **+13** |
| `borderline_r2_bad` | 15 | BAD (all) | 1 | 14 | 15 GOOD / 0 BAD | 14 | **+13** |
| `high_noise` | 15 | BAD (all) | 8 | 7 | 13 GOOD / 2 BAD | 5 – 9 | **−3 to +1** |

`high_noise` is a range because the v1 ground truth records no per-curve truth
curve, so which two of the fifteen the oracle rule would call BAD is not
recoverable; only the 13/2 split from the v2 generator is known.

The mechanism behind the flip: on all 35 curves the ground truth's recorded
`actual_r_squared` is below 0.95 (0.826 – 0.954) while the **pipeline** measured
`fit_r_squared` >= 0.9535 on every one of them. `actual_r_squared` in the v1
ground truth is a whole-trace statistic; the pipeline fits the truncated growth
phase. The v1 label came from the scenario's intent and from that whole-trace
number, not from the curve that was written — which is the defect the new
`label_rule='oracle'` fixes.

Pooled over the curves the pipeline **accepted and the truth calls GOOD**.

| set | n | A R² | A RMSE | mu R² | mu RMSE | lambda R² | lambda RMSE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1, all accepted | 319 | 0.8251 | 0.14185 | 0.9084 | 0.02820 | 0.9867 | 0.91512 |
| v1, **minus the 2 accepted logistic curves** | 317 | **0.9786** | **0.04967** | 0.9236 | 0.02582 | 0.9898 | 0.80578 |

Two curves out of 319 moved the published v1 A-recovery from R² 0.979 to
0.825: `CURVE0419` has true A 1.496 and a fitted A of 3.861. The audit
estimated this pair at 0.969 / 0.059; measured here it is 0.9786 / 0.04967.

```bash
MPLBACKEND=Agg python - <<'PY'
import sys, numpy as np, pandas as pd
sys.path.insert(0, 'synthetic_data')
from validation.matching import match_results
def rec(d, t, p):
    tt = pd.to_numeric(d[t], errors='coerce').values; pp = pd.to_numeric(d[p], errors='coerce').values
    ok = np.isfinite(tt) & np.isfinite(pp); tt, pp = tt[ok], pp[ok]
    return dict(n=len(tt), rmse=float(np.sqrt(((tt-pp)**2).mean())),
                r2=float(1-((tt-pp)**2).sum()/((tt-tt.mean())**2).sum()))
v1 = match_results(pd.read_csv('synthetic_data/benchmark/v1/ground_truth.csv'),
                   pd.read_csv('synthetic_data/benchmark/v1/processing_results.csv'))
tp = v1[v1.gt_is_good & v1.pred_is_good]
print('v1 all accepted      ', rec(tp, 'true_A', 'gompertz_a'))
print('v1 minus logistic    ', rec(tp[tp.scenario != 'logistic_generated'], 'true_A', 'gompertz_a'))
```

## 5. Noise recalibration: what moved in the gate statistics

Measured over every fitted curve in each run (v1 452 rows, v2 1036 rows).

| statistic | v1 mean / median | v2 mean / median | v1 fail rate | v2 fail rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `monotone_fraction` (gate `>= 0.55`) | 0.8642 / 0.9167 | 0.9073 / 1.0000 | 3.76 % | 2.61 % |
| `residual_autocorr` (gate `<= 0.7`) | 0.1471 / 0.0934 | 0.3095 / 0.2755 | 5.75 % | **17.08 %** |

The autocorrelation distribution is the single biggest behavioural change in
the data: v1 residuals were white, so lag-1 autocorrelation sat near zero and
the 0.7 gate almost never fired. v2 residuals are coloured (`ColoredNoise`,
`tau_fast_h=0.5`, `tau_slow_h=7.5`), calibrated to the real plates, and the
gate now rejects 17 % of fitted curves. Some previously-passing GOOD scenarios
fail on it — `diauxic_second_larger` loses 12 curves to `residual_autocorr`
alone. **The 0.7 threshold was not touched**; only the data moved.

```bash
MPLBACKEND=Agg python - <<'PY'
import pandas as pd
for tag, path in [('v1', 'synthetic_data/benchmark/v1/processing_results.csv'),
                  ('v2', 'synthetic_data/benchmark/v2/processing_results.csv')]:
    d = pd.read_csv(path)
    mono = pd.to_numeric(d.monotone_fraction, errors='coerce').dropna()
    auto = pd.to_numeric(d.residual_autocorr, errors='coerce').dropna()
    print(f'{tag}: n={len(mono)} monotone mean {mono.mean():.4f} fail {100*(mono<0.55).mean():.2f}% | '
          f'autocorr mean {auto.mean():.4f} fail {100*(auto>0.7).mean():.2f}%')
```
